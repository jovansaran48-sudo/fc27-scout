    Prices come from FUT.GG's public PS5 price file; GitHub runs the scouts about every 13 minutes, even when your devices are off. Paper trades copy how you'd really trade: every card is listed at its target for 1 day (it sells if the price gets there, even while you're away), drops are only acted on at check-ins about every 2 hours, and unsold cards are sold at market after a day. Real results will still be a little worse. Your cards, sales and rules are saved in this browser; Jarvis also gets a copy so it can alert you when to sell. Every trade is yours to make in the game; nothing here signs in to EA. All profit figures take off EA's 5% tax.
